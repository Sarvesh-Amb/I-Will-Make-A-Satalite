# Az/El roters

## Function
* pair of two moters
* the Az moters move the antenna horizontally
* the el moters move the antenna from 0 degree to 90 degress ( pointing stright up )
* used the allign the antenna wiht the satalite

## Specs and what they mean
* **Azimuth Range** : This tells you how far the horizontal motor can spin (e.g : comman is 450 cuz it gives you 360 + extra 90 so the motor does not jam )
* **Elevation Range** : This tell you how far the moter can tilt, (e.g : 180 degree, allows the motor to go form flat to stright to flat)
* **Wind load Capacity** : this tell you how much surface area the motor can hold against strong winds without jamming
* **Rotation torgue** : This tells you the twisting power of the motor (Kgf-cm)
* **Breaking torque** : This tell you how tightly the gears lock so your antenna does not go out of allignment
