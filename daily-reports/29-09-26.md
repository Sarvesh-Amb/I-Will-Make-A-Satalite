##Hydrogen-VS-Helium
to lauch the weather ballon we can you **Hydrogen** or **Helium** gas, both have pros and cos 

**Hydrogen**
*pros*
* Cheap
* lighter so more lift

*cons*
* flameable
* regulated by government

**Helium**
*pros*
* inert gas so not flameable
* not regulated by gov

*cons*
* expensive
* lesser lift that hydrogen (by 7-8%)
